## What happened

- **Curve:**
  - Return fell from −20 at the start to a flat −155 within ~17 M steps and stayed there.
  - Q mean is flat at −78; α decayed to ~1e-4.
  - Training success was 0 at every point, and DR never left stage 0.
- **Eval** (200 episodes, stage 0, `eval/`):
  - 0 successes.
  - **200/200 episodes end in a STUCK trip**, at a mean of 41 steps.
  - Final die error p50 151 mm: the die never leaves the well.
- **Renders:** the arm folds down beside its base and presses into the table until STUCK fires. The die is never touched.

## Diagnosis

1. **Hard exploration.**
   - The die starts in a recessed well: a flat 23 mm floor, 50° funnel plates rising 22 mm, and a 26 mm rim.
   - A push cannot get it out. Leaving the well needs a grasp and a lift (plus a place, or a drop near the zone).
   - Random SAC exploration never produced a single success, so the success term never appeared in the replay buffer.
   - This is lesson L3 in the plan ("pure RL never discovered placing").
2. **A degenerate optimum.**
   - With no success signal, the critic settled on "end the episode": a STUCK trip pays the terminal `trip_penalty` (−310) once, instead of the distance and time penalty on every step.
   - The run never learned anything better than tripping, and the low entropy then locked it in.
   - Whether the STUCK trips near the table are all genuine (pressing into the table) or partly false (the sim STUCK rule next to the tray) is **not yet checked**.

## Options (for the user; S4.6 allows ≤ 3 attempts, then a review of the residual-RL fallback per plan §10)

| # | Option | What it changes | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Curriculum on the start position**: first start the die on the open table between the tray and the zone (a push task), then move the start step by step into the well | the task's `die_start` becomes a staged distribution; the DR controller advances it like the DR stages | small code change; plan §10 decision (the start set is part of the task spec) |
| B | **Scripted IK seeds** (plan Phase 4, RLPD-style): a scripted grasp-lift-place controller fills part of the replay buffer with successes | needs `rl/scripted.py` for the die and a demo-mixing ratio in FastSAC | medium; the plan already names this route |
| C | **Check the STUCK rule near the table and tray** before any retry | a diagnostic: are the trips genuine? | small; should be done in any case |
| D | Residual RL on a scripted base | plan §10 says fallback only, by user review | large; a decision |

**Recommendation:** C first (cheap), then A + B together for attempt 2.
- A gives the critic successes early.
- B gives it the grasp-lift motion that pushing cannot discover.
- The task's well start stays the target.
